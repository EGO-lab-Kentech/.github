# EGO Lab — Electric Grid Optimization Lab

We model and optimize power grids for the energy transition. Generation
planning, market design, physics-aware operation of distributed resources,
multi-energy coupling — and the optimization algorithms that make these
problems solvable.

Based at **KENTECH** (한국에너지공과대학교) in Naju, Korea, led by
Prof. Jip Kim (김집).

> The full lab — people, papers, projects, teaching, open positions — is at
> **<https://egolab.kentech.ac.kr>**. The repositories below this README are
> what we open-source on GitHub.

### KPG 193

A 193-bus synthetic Korean power grid we maintain for decarbonization
studies: 122 generators, 359 transmission lines, 8,760 hours of demand,
weather, and renewable profiles. Hosted by the AGM Center at KENTECH.

- Code & data — [`agm-center/kpg-testgrid`](https://github.com/agm-center/kpg-testgrid)
- Platform — <https://agm.kentech.ac.kr>
- Questions — <egolab.kpg@gmail.com>
